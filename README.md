## About

Full Stack Developer based in Asunción, Paraguay, with a strong backend focus and almost two years of production experience across three companies. I build systems that **automate processes** and **streamline business operations**: payment gateways, electronic invoicing, B2B platforms, and large-scale e-commerce.

I design REST and GraphQL APIs, write clean and tested code, and ship end to end with CI/CD. I also have hands-on experience integrating **LLMs** (GPT, Claude) into real applications.

## Experience

**Full Stack / Backend Developer, Aemprex** | Jan 2026 – Present
- Building enterprise applications used by 5,000+ users in a Scrum team.
- Optimized complex SQL queries in the COARCO accounting ERP and built its frontend.
- Developed MiFactu, an electronic invoicing platform: REST APIs, a Flutter mobile app, and payment integrations that automate tax documents and collections.

**Full Stack Developer, Central Shop** | Jul 2025 – Jan 2026
- Built and maintained an internal ERP and a large-scale e-commerce platform serving about 10,000 daily users.
- Integrated Pagopar and Bancard payment gateways across backend and frontend flows.
- Wrote automated tests and set up CI/CD pipelines in GitLab.

**Full Stack Developer, DTP Travel Group** | Mar 2025 – Jul 2025
- Maintained the backend of DTP Mundo, a B2B trade marketing platform used by travel agencies in Paraguay and Bolivia.
- Proposed and designed a 4-layer scraping system that collects, validates, and syncs packages, prices, and availability automatically.
