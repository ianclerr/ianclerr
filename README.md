## About

Full Stack Developer based in Asunción, Paraguay, with a strong backend focus and almost two years of production experience across three companies. I build systems that **automate processes** and **streamline business operations**: payment gateways, electronic invoicing, B2B platforms, and large-scale e-commerce.

I design REST and GraphQL APIs, write clean and tested code, and ship end to end with CI/CD. I also have hands-on experience integrating **LLMs** (GPT, Claude) into real applications.
